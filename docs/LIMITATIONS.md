# Public Diagnosis Limitations

This MVP uses public HTTP/HTML evidence only.

- It cannot confirm CMS admin settings, plugin update status, CRM setup, hosting configuration, DNS ownership, analytics account configuration, or conversion data.
- WordPress plugin/version traces are assumptions when detected from public assets.
- Forms are inspected statically and are never submitted.
- PageSpeed and AI summaries are optional and fail gracefully when keys are missing.
- Numeric scores are deterministic and must not be invented by AI.

Use the diagnosis as a discovery and scoping aid, not as a final security, legal, accessibility, or SEO certification.
